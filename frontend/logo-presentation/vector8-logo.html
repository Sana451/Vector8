Why it works

8 = the digit and the truck class

No metaphor to explain — a fleet owner reads "8" and thinks Class 8 before they think "logo."

One line, three readings

The diagonal stroke is a route heading, a trip in progress, and — because it cuts straight through the numeral — the literal vector the name promises.

Trailing ticks

The two short marks at the tail read as motion lines. They're also just enough repetition to hint at three positions on one line — driver, dispatcher, owner — without turning into a diagram.

Legible at 40px

A thick numeral plus one diagonal stroke survives being shrunk to an app-tray icon; most route/map iconography doesn't.
